Support

Something not working as expected? Here are the most common questions. If yours isn't here, write to onelist@lightsidestudio.com and we'll get back to you.

How do I add a task to My Day?

In the Tasks tab, tap the task's sun. To remove it, tap it again, or touch and hold the task in My Day and choose “Remove from My Day”.

What does “In progress” mean?

What you're doing right now. Touch and hold a task in My Day and choose “Mark in progress”: it moves to the very top. When you complete it, it's no longer in progress.

Why did My Day clear?

Because “Clear My Day every night” is on in Settings. Unfinished tasks aren't lost: they stay in their topic. If you'd rather keep them, turn it off.

How do I change a topic's color?

In Tasks, touch and hold the topic and choose edit. Subtopics use their topic's color, a little softer.

How do I share my tasks?

Open a topic, tap “Select”, choose the tasks and tap Share: they come out as formatted text, ready to copy.

Are my tasks saved to iCloud?

Not yet: they're stored only on your device. If you delete the app, they're deleted with it.
